

My packaging proposal for pimple patches uses illustrations of nine Irish wildflowers to spark curiosity and reconnect young adults with native plants.\
\
Each patch contains real flower extracts with healing properties.\
\
The front features a vibrant golden-yellow gorse flower held up by traditional Claddagh hands.\
\
Packaged in biodegradable brown paper and paired with a poster in English and Gaeilge, the design blends skincare with Ireland\'92s heritage.\
\
The flower imagery on the patches becomes conversation starters, turning trend-driven skincare into a quiet revival of lost knowledge.\



We live in a digital age estranged from nature and heritage. In Ireland, over 56% of native plant species have declined in range or abundance. Many young people can recognise countless brand logos but few can identify the wildflowers in their own area.\Worn on the skin, they transform a skincare routine into a walking field guide, prompting passers-by to ask, “What flower is that?” and inviting users to share its name, folklore, and traditional uses. \
